The longer viewBuild a business that carries forward

A BRAND
WITH SOMEWHERE
TO GO.

The development business gives people a reason to remember, trust and consider you across projects. The way you present it, explain your approach, handle an enquiry and support a buyer’s decision all contribute to that relationship.

Collective brings those connections into one commercial view. The question is how today’s work can support the current opportunity while strengthening the business you are building over time.

This is for founders with a clear ambition for their developer brand and the willingness to work closely on how growth happens.

What we would connectOne commercial direction
01 / Direction

The business behind the pipeline.

Clarify the business ambition, customer, offer and priorities. Establish what the next stage of growth needs to achieve and how the current project pipeline supports that direction. Give the work a shared purpose and the decisions a commercial basis.

02 / Brand & trust

A reason to choose and remember you.

Bring the founder’s perspective, development philosophy, proof and customer experience into a coherent brand. Create useful reasons for buyers, agents and other relevant relationships to understand the business, with consistency between what it promises and what people experience.

03 / The buyer journey

Connect interest with the next step.

Connect campaigns and content with landing pages, enquiries, qualification, follow-up and the sales conversation. Examine what happens between first interest and a commercial outcome, including where ownership or information is lost and what could help the customer move forward.

04 / Visibility

Make the work easier to understand.

Agree the definitions, records and reporting needed to make decisions. Build a clearer view of enquiry quality, progression, sales-cycle behaviour and the contribution of the work. Scope the systems and implementation around what the business needs to act on that information.

The right fitA shared commitment to the work

AMBITION.
INFORMATION.
INVOLVEMENT.

We would look for an established development business, a credible project pipeline and an ambition that extends across individual campaigns.

You would need a founder or senior decision-maker involved, resources for the agreed work and a willingness to share relevant sales and marketing information. That access should be sufficient to understand the journey from enquiry to outcome.

The strongest starting point is a shared commercial question and the commitment to investigate it, make decisions and act on what is learned.

The role of StratosphereClear responsibilities

CONNECTED.
CLEARLY DEFINED.

Collective’s focus here is the development business: its direction, brand and connected growth capability across projects.

Stratosphere focuses on property developer lead generation. Where project-level acquisition belongs in the wider plan, we can consider its role and agree the delivery responsibilities. The relationship stays clear about who owns the work and how it contributes to the business.
