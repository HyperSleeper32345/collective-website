UNDERSTAND
THE WHOLE
BUSINESS.

More traffic, more enquiries and more orders can all be useful. Their value depends on what happens around them: the cost of acquisition, the strength of the offer, the experience after the click and the capacity to deliver.

For an ecommerce business, that may mean examining the contribution from each order and the reasons customers return. For a considered purchase, it may mean following an enquiry through qualification, sales conversations and the eventual decision.

We look at those connections before proposing the work. The aim is a practical commercial priority that everyone involved can understand and act on.

From understanding to actionA working sequence
01 / Establish

Agree where the business stands.

We establish the objective, current performance and relevant costs. That includes the information already available, the questions it cannot answer and the practical limits on delivery. A useful baseline gives us a shared starting point; it also makes assumptions visible before they become part of the plan.

02 / Diagnose

Find the constraint.

We examine where the customer journey loses momentum and where commercial value is being left behind. The issue may sit in demand, the offer, conversion, follow-up, retention or operations. We separate observations from hypotheses, then identify what needs to be understood or tested before committing more resources.

03 / Connect

Make one plan.

Strategy, acquisition, content, conversion and customer journeys need clear priorities and responsibilities. We agree what happens first, what each part is intended to achieve and the delivery capacity required. Channels and production volumes follow the scope, with implementation and specialist support defined where needed.

04 / Review

Use the evidence to decide again.

Reporting should lead to a decision. We review what happened, what it means and what should change next. Within the founding partnership, weekly updates, monthly advisory and quarterly strategy provide that rhythm. The measures and review cadence for other engagements are agreed around the work.
