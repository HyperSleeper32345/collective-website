How we look at the workThe number is the beginning

WHAT CHANGED.
WHY IT MATTERS.

Good evidence connects an outcome to the situation that made it meaningful. A larger sales figure can be useful. So can a clearer decision about which products to promote, which costs to question or where the customer journey needs attention.

That is the perspective we bring to a case study: the business question, the work and the limits of what the result tells us.

01 / Outcome

Be specific about the measure.

Revenue, profit, repeat purchase and operating costs answer different questions. The label and the period belong beside the number.

02 / Contribution

Make the roles clear.

Client decisions, specialist expertise and systems can all contribute. Credit should reflect the people and partners involved.

03 / Context

Keep the next business in view.

A previous result can inform a conversation. The next engagement still needs its own baseline, priorities and commercial terms.
