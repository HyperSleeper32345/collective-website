Collective eCommerce

Make profit measurable.

Collective eCommerce is a profit measurement suite, built to show what each order earns and audit a store's Meta account before spend changes.

Book a Strategy Call View the System

Revenue does not show whether an order made money.

Most reporting for online stores stops at revenue and the conversions each advertising platform counts. Neither shows what an order earned after product, pick and pack, shipping, payment, and advertising costs.

Collective eCommerce is built to close that gap. It measures contribution margin per order, the profit left after those costs.

What's included.

Contribution Margin Measurement

Contribution margin per order, measured from the store's Shopify data and advertising spend.

Meta Account Audit

A read-only review of the store's Meta account, completed before any change to spend.

Contribution Margin Dashboard

A single dashboard that shows contribution margin, acquisition cost, stock cover and cash cycle together.

What we measure.

Contribution Margin per Order

Profit left on each order after every variable cost, including advertising.

Customer Acquisition Cost

Advertising spend per first-time customer, counted from the store's own orders.

Lifetime Value

Gross profit from one customer across every order they place.

Weeks of Cover

Stock on hand, measured in weeks of sales.

Cash Conversion Cycle

Days between paying for stock and being paid for it.

A platform's conversion count is not the store's order count.

Meta credits sales to its ads by its own attribution rules, so its conversion count can differ from the store's own orders. A store that sets its budget on that count can pay for sales it never made.

The Meta account audit is designed to check that count. It uses read-only access, needs no new advertising spend, and compares Meta's figures with the store's Shopify orders.

Health and wellness businesses that sell online.

Collective eCommerce is the right starting point when a health or wellness store cannot see what each order earns after costs. It fits stores that sell on Shopify and advertise on Meta.

Atone Skin Clinic, a Collective client, already has the contribution margin dashboard.

Start with the constraint, not the spend.

Most stores assume the answer is more advertising spend. Sometimes that is true. Often the constraint is contribution margin, attribution, or stock.

Collective starts by identifying the actual constraint, then scopes the parts that measure it. Each part is priced on its own, and Collective agrees the scope and price before any work begins.
