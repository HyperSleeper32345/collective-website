eCommerce

Make profit measurable.

eCommerce is Collective's profit measurement suite, built to show what each order makes and to audit your Meta account before any change to spend.

Book a Strategy Call View the System

Revenue does not show whether an order made money.

Most eCommerce reporting stops at revenue and the conversions each advertising platform counts. Neither shows what an order earned once product, shipping, payment and advertising costs are paid.

Collective eCommerce is built to close that gap. It measures contribution margin per order from your own Shopify store and your advertising spend, so every decision starts from what an order made.

What we measure.

Contribution Margin per Order

Revenue less cost of goods, shipping, payment fees, pick and pack, and advertising, divided by orders.

Customer Acquisition Cost

Advertising spend divided by the first-time customers your store recorded.

Lifetime Value

Average order value, multiplied by purchase frequency, multiplied by gross margin.

Payback Period

Days of contribution margin needed to recover the cost of one new customer.

Weeks of Cover

Current inventory divided by weekly sales.

Cash Conversion Cycle

Days stock is held, plus days until payment arrives, less the days your suppliers allow.

Read the account before changing the spend.

Advertising platforms count conversions through their own attribution, and they count more than your store records. Collective counts new customers in Shopify and checks Meta's figures against your own order records.

The Meta account audit is designed to come first. It reads twelve months of your account with read-only access and needs no new advertising spend. Every recommendation in it names the metric it rests on.
