Before we talk

A FEW
DETAILS.

What exactly is included?+

The strategy, execution, content and reporting in the agreed scope. We define channels, production volumes, systems work and responsibilities before starting. The engagement is prioritised around the business’s needs and the delivery capacity agreed.

How do you measure progress?+

We agree the baseline, relevant costs, commercial targets and reporting method. That lets us assess what growth contributes to the business, including the marketing and delivery costs that affect what you keep.

What does the diagnostic require?+

We agree what information and access are needed first. This may include store and marketing data, product and fulfilment costs, returns, sales history and operational capacity. Access is limited to what the assessment needs. The diagnostic is included for qualified founding applicants.

Can the work move in-house later?+

We can discuss a staged transition when your business and team are ready. Training, handover, systems support and ongoing advisory would be scoped and agreed separately.
